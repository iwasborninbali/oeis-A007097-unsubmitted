# Prior art and credit

On 2026-11-13, `https://oeis.org/search?q=id:A007097&fmt=text` returned one entry with Ordowski's 2015 conjecture. The reviewed answer cites Mario Cipolla (1902), *La determinazione asintotica dell’n-esimo numero primo*, Rendiconti dell'Accademia delle Scienze Fisiche e Matematiche di Napoli, series 3, 8, 132–166, for the nth-prime expansion, and Christian Axler, *New estimates for the nth prime number*, arXiv:1706.03651 (2017), for a modern presentation. It reports that a 2020 MathOverflow argument was explicitly withdrawn. It describes Pierre Dusart, *Estimates of Some Functions Over Primes without R.H.*, arXiv:1002.0442 (2010), as providing nth-prime estimates; Robert G. Batchko, *A prime fractal and global quasi-self-similar structure in the distribution of prime-indexed primes*, arXiv:1405.2900 (2014), as concerning iterated prime-indexed sequences; and Dominique Manchon, *Une remarque sur l'arborification de Matula*, arXiv:2602.03150 (2026), as concerning Matula arborification. These descriptions follow the reviewed answer and the papers were not independently re-read here. Priority for the deduction has not been established exhaustively.
